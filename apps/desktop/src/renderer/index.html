How it works

  1. This app is your home on the network. Your profile and posts are stored on this computer and shared directly with other people. No company holds them.
  2. Post and browse in your browser. Click Open SocialKey to write posts, edit your profile, and read your timeline.
  3. Follow people by their ID. Ask friends for their SocialKey ID, paste it into the search box on the home page, then click Follow on their profile.
  4. Keep it running. Your followers keep your recent posts available while you're offline, but posting needs this app. Closing this window keeps SocialKey running in the tray.

Friends keep each other online

This app keeps a copy of the latest posts from people you follow, so their profiles stay available while their computers are off. Your followers do the same for you.

Use SocialKey on your phone

Post and read from a phone or tablet on the same Wi-Fi. This computer stays the one holding your key.

Scan with your phone's camera to open SocialKey there, already connected. Each code works once.

Back up your account

Your account is a secret key stored on this computer. Its recovery phrase is the only way to get your account back if this computer is lost. Write it down and keep it somewhere safe and private.

Restore an account from a recovery phrase

This replaces the account on this computer. The current account's files are archived, not deleted.

Using a different SocialKey website?

Any SocialKey website can post through this app. On that site, choose Connect and enter this pairing code. Each code works once; a new one appears after it's used.
